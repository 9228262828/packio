PACKIO PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 21, 2026

Summary: PACKIO is an offline-first packing list and trip organizer. The current core version does not require an account, Firebase, a remote backend, cloud sync, ads, or behavioral analytics.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how PACKIO, package com.packio.tripplanner, handles information when you use the app.

2. Scope

This Policy applies to the current core version of PACKIO and these informational pages.

3. Offline-First Design

PACKIO is designed as an offline-first trip and packing organizer. The current core functionality does not require an internet connection.

4. Trip Data

PACKIO may store trip names, destinations, start dates, end dates, and trip notes locally on your device.

5. Packing List Data

PACKIO may store packing item names, categories, quantities, packed status, essential-item status, and item notes locally on your device.

6. Categories

Packing items may be organized into categories such as Clothes, Documents, Electronics, Toiletries, Medicines, Work, and Other.

7. Packed Status

PACKIO stores whether a packing item is marked packed or unpacked so it can calculate progress.

8. Essential Items

PACKIO may store whether an item is marked essential so important items can be visually highlighted.

9. Quantities

The app may store quantities for individual packing items.

10. Trip Notes

Notes entered for a trip are stored locally and are used only to display the information back to you inside PACKIO.

11. Item Notes

Notes entered for packing items are stored locally and used only to support your packing list.

12. Trip Dates

Start and end dates are stored locally for trip organization and upcoming-trip display.

13. Destination Information

Destination names entered by you are stored locally and are not used for location tracking.

14. Packing Progress

PACKIO calculates packing progress locally from the number of packed and total items.

15. Search

Search is performed locally against saved trip names and destination text.

16. Trip Duplication

When you duplicate a trip, PACKIO creates a local copy of the trip and its packing items with packed status reset.

17. Templates

Ready-made packing templates are built into the app. Choosing a template adds predefined items to the selected trip.

18. Statistics

PACKIO may calculate local statistics such as total trips, total items, packed items, essential items, and overall packing progress.

19. Dark Mode Preference

Your dark-mode preference may be stored locally so PACKIO can remember your selected appearance.

20. Local Storage

The current core version stores data using local application storage such as SharedPreferences.

21. No Account Required

You do not need to create a PACKIO account, username, password, or profile to use the current core version.

22. No Login Data

Because the current core version has no login system, PACKIO does not request account credentials.

23. No Firebase

The current core version is not designed to use Firebase for authentication, cloud databases, analytics, or remote configuration.

24. No Remote Backend

The current core version does not send your saved trips or packing lists to a PACKIO-operated remote backend.

25. No Cloud Sync

The current core version does not provide PACKIO-operated cloud synchronization between devices.

26. No Advertising

The current core version does not contain PACKIO-operated advertising.

27. No Behavioral Analytics

The current core version is not designed to collect behavioral analytics for advertising, profiling, or marketing.

28. No Cross-App Tracking

PACKIO is not designed to track your activity across unrelated third-party apps or websites.

29. No Sale of Personal Information

PACKIO does not intentionally sell or rent the locally stored trip and packing information described in this Policy.

30. Permissions

The described core features do not require access to precise location, camera, microphone, contacts, phone, SMS, calendar, photos, media, files, or payment information.

31. Location

PACKIO does not require precise or approximate device location for the current core features.

32. Destination Is Not Location Tracking

Typing a destination into a trip is simply saved text. It does not grant PACKIO access to your device location.

33. Camera

The current core version does not require camera access.

34. Microphone

The current core version does not require microphone access or audio recording.

35. Contacts

The current core version does not require access to your contacts.

36. Phone and SMS

The current core version does not require phone-call, call-log, or SMS permissions.

37. Calendar

The current core version does not require device calendar access. Trip dates are entered and stored inside PACKIO.

38. Photos and Media

The current core version does not require access to your photos, gallery, or media library.

39. Files and Documents

The current core version does not require access to personal files or documents for normal use.

40. Payments

The current core version does not require payment-card details, banking credentials, or payment account information.

41. No Booking Services

PACKIO does not process flight, hotel, transport, or other travel bookings in the current core version.

42. No Passport Uploads

The current core version does not require you to upload passports, IDs, visas, tickets, or travel documents.

43. Sensitive Information

Do not use free-form notes to store passwords, payment credentials, private keys, or other highly sensitive information.

44. Data Retention

Locally stored trip and packing data generally remains on your device until you delete a trip, reset app data, clear application storage, uninstall the app, or the operating system removes the data.

45. Delete a Trip

You can delete an individual trip and its packing list from within the app.

46. Reset All Data

PACKIO includes a reset option that deletes saved trips and packing information from the app's local storage.

47. Uninstalling the App

Uninstalling PACKIO may remove locally stored information, subject to operating-system or device backup and restore behavior.

48. Device Backups

Your operating system or device provider may back up application data according to your device settings. Such backups are not controlled by PACKIO's core app logic.

49. Data Recovery

Because the current core version does not include PACKIO-operated cloud backup, we may not be able to recover deleted trips or packing lists.

50. Device Security

You are responsible for protecting access to your device using available security features such as a passcode, PIN, password, or biometric lock.

51. Children

PACKIO is a general-purpose organization tool and is not designed to collect personal information from children through accounts, profiles, forms, messaging, or social features.

52. Third-Party Platforms

App stores and device platforms may independently process technical, account, security, purchase, or diagnostic information under their own privacy policies.

53. Google Play

If you obtain PACKIO through Google Play, Google may process app-installation, account, device, security, purchase, or diagnostic information under Google's own policies and terms.

54. GitHub Pages

These informational pages may be hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub may process ordinary web-server information according to its own policies. These pages do not require you to submit PACKIO trip data.

55. Support Communications

If you contact us at associatebusiness221@gmail.com, your email provider and our email provider will process the information you voluntarily include. Do not send passwords, payment credentials, secret keys, or other highly sensitive information.

56. Legal Requirements

If legally required, information voluntarily provided through support communications may be disclosed to comply with applicable law or valid legal process.

57. International Use

You may use PACKIO in different countries. Local app data remains on your device in the current core version, while app stores, email providers, and website hosts may process their own data in other jurisdictions.

58. Future Features

If future releases add accounts, cloud sync, analytics, crash reporting, advertising, travel bookings, payments, location features, online services, sharing, or additional permissions, this Policy should be reviewed and updated before release.

59. Your Choices

You can use dark mode, delete individual trips, reset all local data, clear app storage, uninstall PACKIO, or stop using the app.

60. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when PACKIO changes or when legal, technical, or app-store requirements change.

61. Contact

For privacy questions about PACKIO, contact associatebusiness221@gmail.com.
